Why sell to a specialist rather than a marketplace

Listing a big collection yourself means photographing hundreds of cards, wearing the fees, posting dozens of parcels, and handling every dispute that follows. Selling to a pawn shop or a general second-hand dealer usually means being offered a flat price by someone who can't tell a 1st Edition Machamp from an unlimited one. We price against real Australian sold data, we tell you which cards are carrying the value, and we pay for the whole lot at once.

If you'd rather sell the good cards yourself and just move the rest, say so — we'll quote on the bulk and tell you honestly which pieces are worth your time listing.

Common questions about selling a collection

How much are my Pokemon cards worth?

Most collections are carried by a handful of cards. Send photos and we'll identify the pieces that matter and value them against recent Australian sold prices, not overseas asking prices. The valuation is free whether you sell to us or not.

Do you buy inherited or deceased estate collections?

Yes, and regularly. We'll go through it patiently, explain what's there, and give you a written offer. If you're an executor or solicitor and need a valuation figure for an estate rather than a sale, we can help with that too.

Do I have to post my cards to get an offer?

No. The valuation happens from photos. Nothing moves until you've accepted an offer, and for larger Adelaide collections we can come to you.

How fast do I get paid?

Offer within 24 hours of receiving your photos. Payment by bank transfer or cash on collection once the cards are confirmed as described.

Where in Australia do you buy from?

Everywhere. We're an Adelaide business and can meet locally across South Australia, and we buy from collectors in Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Perth and regional Australia using insured, tracked postage.
