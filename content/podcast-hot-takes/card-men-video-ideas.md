### 1. "PSA grading is dead?" — the headline take ⭐ (best first video)
- **Clip:** 0:00 cold open + 18:43 ("not even worth grading through PSA anymore").
- **Angle:** Half-right. Dead for bulk; at the high end the slab IS the asset.
- **Format:** Stitch + React. Hook: *"A podcast just said PSA grading isn't worth it anymore — and they're half right. The half they got wrong is where all the money is."*
- Full script already written in `card-men-psa-grading-hot-take.md`.

### 2. "They accidentally explained why slabs are about to moon" 📈
- **Clip:** 18:50–19:54 — they reason: grading costs ↑ → fewer cards graded → less supply → graded value ↑.
- **Angle:** Pure investor framing. *They* made your bull case for already-graded blue chips.
- **Format:** Talking-head explainer over their audio. Hook: *"Two vendors just accidentally explained why your sealed PSA 10s are about to go up — let me draw it out."* Use a simple supply/price arrow graphic.

### 3. "Watch PSA's shovels, not card prices" 🏗️
- **Clip:** 7:40–8:01 ("if they break ground on a new facility, that's them saying this is here to stay").
- **Angle:** The real bull signal isn't hype — it's PSA capex. Smart-money watch-this framing.
- **Format:** Myth-buster / explainer. Hook: *"Everyone's watching card prices. The signal that actually matters is whether PSA pours concrete."*

### 4. "A vendor admitted you'll NEVER find a 10 in the case" 🔍
- **Clip:** 29:32–30:13 and 36:00–37:00 (the gem-mint chases get siphoned before they hit the case).
- **Angle:** Insider admission → why buying *already-graded* high-end beats chasing raws at shows.
- **Format:** Stitch + React. Hook: *"A card vendor just told on himself: the PSA-10 Charizard is never in the case. Here's why that makes buying graded the smarter play."*

### 5. "Can you make passive money grading? Two vendors fought for 20 minutes" 🥊
- **Clip:** 24:43–44:00 — Morgan vs Mert debate (grade-for-10s vs "you need obsession + capital").
- **Angle:** Referee it. Both miss that for a time-poor newcomer, buying a few blue-chip slabs beats grinding shows.
- **Format:** Split-screen "scorecard." Hook: *"Two pros argued for 20 minutes about getting into cards and both missed the obvious answer."* ✅/❌ overlays.

### 6. "Tom Brady opening 12 card shops isn't hype — it's institutional money" 🏈
- **Clip:** 46:42–47:14 and 51:02–51:18 (celebrity chain; "people view these as legitimate assets… like McDonald's franchises").
- **Angle:** Bullish macro read — corporate/celebrity capital entering = high-end legitimized. (Or run the contrarian "is this a top signal?" version.)
- **Format:** Talking-head + B-roll of your high-end slabs. Hook: *"When Tom Brady opens his 12th card store, that's not a celebrity cash-grab — that's Wall Street energy hitting the hobby."*

### 7. "$500 MILLION sitting in a backlog" 💰
- **Clip:** 2:03 (10M-card backlog) + 6:09–6:43 (the $250M–$500M math).
- **Angle:** Make the number visceral; what a half-billion-dollar grading bottleneck means for slab scarcity.
- **Format:** Receipts/data. Hook: *"There's half a BILLION dollars frozen in PSA's backlog right now. Here's what that does to slab prices."* Big number on screen.

### 8. "Mom-and-pop vs corporate card chains" 🏪
- **Clip:** 51:02–51:36 and 56:39–57:30 (chains vs mom-and-pop; cards as legitimate assets).
- **Angle:** Where High End Fire fits — the curated collector-investor lane corporate chains can't serve.
- **Format:** Talking-head opinion. Hook: *"Corporate card chains are coming. Here's the one thing they can never do — and it's exactly what I do."*

## Quick-pick guidance
- **Make #1 first** (script's done, strongest hook, most universal).
- **#2 and #4** are the most on-brand for the "data-driven collector-investor" positioning.
- **#6** has the broadest reach (Tom Brady = non-Pokémon audience discovery).
- Reusable series hook for all of these: **"Card Men said WHAT?"** — one spicy podcast take per short, with the High End Fire ruling.

## Production reminders
- Hook in ≤2s; lead on the claim, not an intro.
- Show a real slab on every value claim (your PSA 10 Stormfront Charizard, graded Mewtwo).
- Keep their clip to 3–5s and tag "@ Card Men Podcast" — boosts reach, avoids looking like a subtweet, opens a collab door.
- Subtitle everything (most viewers watch muted).
