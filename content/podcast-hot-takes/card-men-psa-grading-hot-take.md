## 5. Production notes

- **Hook is everything** — the first line must land in 2s or the short dies. Lead on
  "PSA grading is dead… they're half right," not on a slow intro.
- **Show the slabs.** Every claim about high-end value needs a real PSA 10 on screen
  (use existing assets in `/images` or shoot fresh case footage).
- **Credit lightly.** A 1–2s clip or on-screen "@ Card Men Podcast" reaction framing
  boosts reach and avoids looking like a subtweet. Don't over-clip their video.
- **Alt angle if you'd rather agree:** "A podcast finally said it — stop grading bulk.
  Here's the only 3 cards I'd still send to PSA." Same hook energy, different payoff.
