## SPIN-OFF EPISODES (same research, four more videos)

**#2 — "The 90s you remember never existed"**
The washed-out watercolour scans everyone grew up with were inaccurate reproductions; the true colours of Sugimori's originals were only recovered recently from an unearthed Japanese guidebook. So the "90s Pokémon look" is partly a printing artifact — a memory of a bad scan. Uninori is drawing a feeling, not a style. Deeply shareable, slightly philosophical.

**#3 — "Nostalgia is now a product category"**
Card Ladder's Pokémon index is up ~116% YoY and the market's a multi-billion-dollar ecosystem built on 30-somethings buying their childhood back. Hiring artists who draw retro isn't an accident — it's targeting. Spicy, argumentative, big comment section. Land it warm, not cynical: it works because the art is genuinely good.

**#4 — "The cheapest great art in the hobby"**
Uninori's Meowth IR sits around $22 raw. The Raging Bolt ex SIR ~$70. TR's Nidoking ex SIR ~$100. Compare to a Kanda alt where you pay a premium for the signature alone. Blends the art pillar into your proven ranking/liquidity format that hit on TikTok.

**#5 — "He drew his hero's favourite Pokémon"**
Uninori drew Hoot-Hoot for TCG Pocket. Hoot-Hoot is Ken Sugimori's own favourite Pokémon. Also: Uninori's stated favourite trainer battle theme is Johto's — Hoot-Hoot's home region. Short, warm, 45s max.

## FACT SHEET (verify before publishing)

- Debut: Grotle, Scarlet & Violet—Temporal Forces. JP 26 Jan 2024, EN 22 Mar 2024.
- ~24 main TCG cards + ~6 TCG Pocket cards as of Jul 2026.
- Influences: Ken Sugimori, Kagemaru Himeno, Akira Toriyama.
- Pokémon.com described the style as evoking 90s manga/anime industry art.
- Socials: X @uninori550, Tumblr uninori.tumblr.com, Skeb @uninori550.
- Meowth 106/094 Phantasmal Flames IR — background is Gen 1 Kanto, near Cerulean City.
- ⚠️ CHECK: Bulbapedia's illustration-contest page lists Uninori among Grand Prix entrants the
  panel deemed excellent, who were then approached by Creatures Inc. If accurate, "six years of
  rejection" is a simplification — closer to "noticed in 2020, waited until 2024 for a pack."
  Either verify or use the softer line: "years of submitting before a card ever shipped."
- ⚠️ DO NOT USE ON CAMERA: Toriyama died 1 Mar 2024, three weeks before the EN debut set.
  The art was finished long before (JP set was already out). Coincidence only — using it as
  a poignant beat reads as manufactured if anyone checks the dates.
