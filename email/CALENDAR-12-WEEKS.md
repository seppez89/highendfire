## Notes on running the calendar

- **Week 6 and Week 11 carry the commercial load.** If a quarter has to earn revenue,
  those are the two to put effort into.
- **Week 9 has no product and that is not negotiable.** It's the issue that makes the
  other eleven credible.
- **Swap freely for something real.** If you buy a collection, take a card at the show,
  or have a genuinely good week at the table, that story beats anything scheduled here.
  Move the planned issue down a week. Real beats planned every time.
- **After Week 12, loop the structure, not the content.** Same six themes, new cards,
  new artists. The series is the format, not the list.
