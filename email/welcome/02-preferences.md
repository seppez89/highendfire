# WELCOME 2 — preferences and segmentation

**Delay:** +4 days after Welcome 0 (2 days after Welcome 1)
**Goal:** set the segment tag with one click. No form, no fields.

---

## How the tagging works (set this up once in Kit)

Each of the six links below is a **link trigger**: clicking it applies a tag and lands the
reader on a real, useful page. Nothing else happens — no confirmation screen, no "thanks
for your preferences!" page. The reader thinks they clicked a link; you got a segment.

| Link | Applies tag | Lands on |
|---|---|---|
| The art | `art` | Site, illustration/alt-art cards |
| Graded slabs | `graded` | Site, filtered to graded |
| Sealed | `sealed` | Site, sealed products |
| The high end | `highend` | Site, $1,000+ |
| Under $200 | `starter` | Site, affordable band |
| Dragon Ball / One Piece | `tcg-other` | Site, non-Pokémon |

Anyone who clicks nothing stays `art` — the correct default, because that's the brand.
**Do not chase non-clickers with a second email.** One ask.

Quick one, and it genuinely changes what turns up in your inbox.

Collectors are not one group. The person hunting a specific illustrator's work and the
person building a graded run of one character want almost nothing in common, and sending
them the same email every week serves neither.

So — **click whichever of these sounds most like you.** You don't have to fill anything in.
One click and I'll know.

→ **[The art]([[LINK]])** — I'm here for the illustration. The artist, the details, the
cards that are genuinely good pictures.

→ **[Graded slabs]([[LINK]])** — I buy in holders. Grades, certs, condition.

→ **[Sealed]([[LINK]])** — Boxes and packs. I'd rather it stayed shut.

→ **[The high end]([[LINK]])** — Show me the serious pieces. I'll wait for the right one.

→ **[Under $200]([[LINK]])** — I collect steadily, not expensively. Good cards, sane prices.

→ **[Dragon Ball / One Piece]([[LINK]])** — Not just Pokémon.

Click more than one if more than one is true.

**And if you're in South Australia** — [tell me]([[LINK / tag: sa-local]]). I'll be at a
show soon, and it's worth knowing who might come and say hello.

Either way, the Thursday letter is the same letter for everybody. This just changes which
cards I point at in it, and stops me showing you $12,000 slabs if that's not what you're
here for.

— Jonathon
High End Fire Collectables

*P.S. Still worth doing if you haven't: reply with the one card you're hunting. The want
list is how most of what I buy gets chosen.*

## Notes
- Six options is the ceiling. Add a seventh and click rate drops because it becomes a form.
- Every option is written in the reader's voice ("I'd rather it stayed shut"), not as a
  category label. People click the one that sounds like them, not the one that's accurate.
- The `sa-local` tag is the one that will matter most in August. Cheap to collect now.
