YOU’RE IN

You’re on the list. Here’s exactly what that means, in four lines, so nothing about this is a surprise later.

Every Thursday evening you’ll get one email called Raking Light. One card, or one idea about cards, examined properly. It takes about ninety seconds to read.

Raking light is what a conservator uses on a painting — a lamp held almost flat against the surface, so every brushstroke and crack jumps out. It’s the same move you already make when you tilt a card under a lamp to check an edge. That’s the whole idea here: these things are worth looking at the way art is looked at.

What you won’t get: price predictions, market news, “buy now before it moons”, or three emails a week when something goes on sale. One email. Thursday. That’s it.
