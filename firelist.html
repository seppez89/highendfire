QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up and no subscription. If the shared card reader is busy you can add your own free Google AI key in Settings, or pick cards from the official list yourself.

Which cards does it work with?

Pokémon, One Piece, Dragon Ball, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic and Lorcana, raw or graded (PSA, BGS, CGC and more). English Pokémon cards are also checked against the official card database.

Does it list straight to eBay?

It makes a Seller Hub bulk-upload file. In eBay go to Seller Hub → Reports → Uploads and choose the file. Whatnot, Shopify and WooCommerce work the same way through their import screens.

What happens to my photos?

They’re kept for 14 days so the marketplace can copy them when you import, then deleted automatically. Location data is stripped on upload. Full privacy details.
